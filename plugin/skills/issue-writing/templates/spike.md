# Spike template

*Guidance in italics. Delete guidance as the spike is filled. Delete optional sections with nothing to say.*

*A Spike reduces uncertainty. It does not promise production behaviour. A prototype, benchmark, or configuration is in scope only when needed to answer the question; production adoption or remediation normally becomes follow-up work.*

## Question

*State one primary question or falsifiable hypothesis. The Spike is finished when this is answered as far as the timebox and available evidence allow. Phrase it so every answer that would change the decision is available, doing nothing included.*

## Why it matters

*Name the product, delivery, timing, commercial, or technical decision that will use the answer, and what each answer would lead to. If no decision changes, the research may not justify a Spike — say so when the kind is yours to pick; when the user asked for a Spike, keep drafting and carry the missing decision as a `[QUESTION]`.*

## Timebox

**Duration or end date:**

*Use an explicit duration or date. “Timeboxed” without a value is incomplete. Reaching the timebox is a valid exit even when the result is inconclusive.*

## Context

*Systems, scenarios, constraints, and measurements to investigate. Where the Spike chooses between known options, list them without verdicts and say whether the list is complete. Where finding the options is the work, name the starting points instead (systems, prior art, people to ask) and leave the answer open. A claim about an option's cost, strength, or fit is an `[ASSUMPTION]` for the Spike to test, or waits for the closeout.*
*Questions whose answers contribute directly to the primary question. Do not let this become an unrelated research backlog.*

## Deliverables

*Specify what must exist at the end. Prefer concrete evidence: measurements with conditions, observed samples, compared options, prototype results, decision record, or documented limitations.*

-

## Risks / Assumptions / Dependencies

*Label assumptions that could invalidate the result. Record required access, external expertise, representative data, owning-team input, or safety constraints. Tag an undecided rule `[QUESTION]` and an unmet coordination, access or ownership need `[DEPENDENCY]`, so both block here as they do on every other kind.*

-

## References

*Link source material, related decisions, and tickets that will consume the result. Say why each reference matters.*

# Closeout

*Fill the sections below during or at the end of the Spike. They are not required in the initial draft.*

## Answer / Decision

*Give the concise answer to the primary question and the decision it supports. Where the research produced a page or report, the answer and the recommendation still belong here — link the page for the evidence behind them, so a reader who cannot open it still knows what was decided.*

## Findings and Evidence

*Record observations, measurements, option comparisons, and limitations. Keep enough context for another person to interpret or reproduce the evidence.*

## Recommendation

*State what should happen next and why. Name each piece of follow-up work — adopting an artifact, fixing what the Spike found — as a follow-up ticket here; none of it is reported as done by this Spike.*

## Produced Artifacts

*Link any ADR, report, benchmark evidence, prototype, saved sample, or other durable output. A prototype is evidence unless separately accepted as production work.*
